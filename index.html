About Us

Since 1978, we have been crafting a legacy of trust, quality, and excellence in the diamond industry. Starting with a passion for perfection, our journey has been built on strong relationships and word-of-mouth referrals from satisfied customers across the globe. Specialising in diamond manufacturing, wholesaling, and creating stunning diamond jewellery, we bring brilliance to life. Every diamond is a testament to our dedication to quality, precision, and timeless beauty. Our jewellery reflects not just elegance but the stories and dreams of our valued clients. We take pride in offering diamonds certified by the International Gemological Institute (IGI), ensuring the highest standards of authenticity and quality. With IGI certification, our customers enjoy complete peace of mind, knowing their diamonds are graded by experts for their brilliance, cut, and clarity. With decades of experience and a reputation that speaks for itself, we proudly serve a diverse clientele, including non-resident Indians (NRIs) who appreciate the artistry and integrity we stand for. Our unwavering commitment to excellence has allowed us to grow steadily, guided by the trust and satisfaction of our customers. "Where certified quality meets affordability, and brilliance shines with reliability."

I arrived in Mumbai with dreams and determination at the age of 13, starting as a humble diamond polisher. Through dedication and perseverance, I built Krishna Diamond into a thriving business. Today, I am proud of our journey and encourage everyone to pursue their passions with unwavering commitment and hard work. Believe in yourself!

As a well-established businessman in Vadodara, my journey has been one of dedication and integrity. Playing a significant role in Krishna Diamond's growth has been a rewarding experience. I urge everyone to strive for excellence and contribute positively to their communities. Success follows those who work hard and stay true to their values.
